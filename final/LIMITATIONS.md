# Limitations: sources of uncertainty and possible bias

Summary sheet for the final report (as of 2026-11-11). Details and checks: `results/audit_report.md`,
`R/90_audit_checks.R`. All numbers are validation results (May–Jun 2025); the Jul–Sep test period is untouched.

| # | Limitation | What it affects | Likely direction of bias | Evidence / size | Mitigation and status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Validation set reused for decisions** (kept the network features, RF hyperparameters, widened tuning grid, final-study feature design) | All validation numbers, especially tuned RF and the chosen features | Optimistic: validation AP/Brier look better than on new data | Clean RF AP 0.463 untuned vs 0.483 tuned at 2 h | Freeze features, settings and the list of sensitivity analyses in a written plan; evaluate once on Jul–Sep. Midway slides label numbers as validation and the RF as untuned. *To do.* |
| 2 | **Timing assumptions: information is known the moment it happens** (BTS outcomes for historical, same-day, recent, network and rotation features; METAR at observation time) | Clean-model performance; size of the C − W/WO gaps | Optimistic for the clean model if the user is a traveller relying on public data (BTS released monthly, ~2 months later); roughly right for an airline/airport operations user | Not yet measured | Name the intended user (operations) in the report; weather reporting-lag sensitivity at 15/30 min (in progress); BTS public-release-lag sensitivity. *Partly to do.* |
| 3 | **Population limited to flights that operated** (cancelled and diverted flights excluded), 10 hubs (closed network), validation in May–Jun | External validity of every result | Results describe completed flights between large hubs in early summer; cancellations cluster on the most disrupted days, so real-world difficulty is understated | Eligible flights: 234,307 of 237,138 closed-network dev flights; validation delay rate 0.267–0.280 vs training 0.202 | State the population explicitly; do not generalise to cancellations, other airports or other seasons. Test period (Jul–Sep) adds a second season. *Reporting only.* |
| 4 | **Confidence intervals are conditional** | Width of every CI | CIs too narrow for statements about other seasons or a refitted model: they cover day-to-day variation within 61 validation days with the models held fixed, not training variability or seasonal change | 1-day vs 7-day block bootstrap gave essentially the same CIs at midway; many comparisons are reported | Describe CIs as conditional on the fitted models and evaluation period; add the 7-day-block check to the final study; avoid over-reading marginal differences. *Partly to do.* |
| 5 | **Calibration differs by model** | Brier comparisons between LR and RF; probability interpretation | All models under-predict the delay rate (seasonal shift); RF probabilities are too conservative, so part of an LR-vs-RF Brier difference is calibration, not ranking | Mean prediction 0.22–0.24 (LR), 0.24–0.26 (RF) vs 0.267 observed; calibration slope ≈ 0.9–1.0 (LR), ≈ 1.4–1.5 (RF); intercept shift changes Brier by ≤ 0.002 | Report AP (ranking) and Brier together; recalibrate RF with Platt/isotonic fitted on data separate from the evaluation data. *To do.* |
| 6 | **Minor data issues** | A handful of feature values | Negligible | 3 flights departed > 30 min early, so at 1 h / 30 min their own departure enters the same-day origin count; 18 legs delayed > 22 h get a departure time off by one day | Exclude a flight from its own same-day count; use `DepDelay` for very long delays. *To do (no effect on conclusions expected).* |
| 7 | **Aircraft swaps recorded under the final tail number** (final extension only) | Rotation features, especially at long prediction times | Optimistic: swaps follow disruptions, so the recorded rotation carried post-cutoff information | 2.6% of flights; delay rate 0.68 vs 0.22; rotation gain at 12 h (LR) 0.049 → 0.024 after the fix | **Fixed**: main analysis uses regular-rotation flights for training and evaluation (`results/regular/`); all flights kept as a sensitivity (`results/all_flights/`). Remaining assumption: for regular rotations the recorded aircraft is the planned one; same-day-legs-only sensitivity *to do*. |

**How to word conclusions.** Gains from leaked or later information are apparent improvements specific to
these fitted models, this population and this evaluation period; they are not evidence of better real-world
prediction. Final claims should come from the single, frozen evaluation on Jul–Sep 2025.
